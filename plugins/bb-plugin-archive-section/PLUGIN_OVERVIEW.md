Open archived threads from the sidebar without changing the active list.

## What you get

- An Archive row just above the sidebar toolbar.
- Click the row to show archived threads above it. Click it again to hide them.
- Click a title to open that thread. Opening a thread leaves it archived.

## How it works

The list reads BB's own archived thread data only while the section is open.
The plugin stores nothing and sends nothing off the machine.
